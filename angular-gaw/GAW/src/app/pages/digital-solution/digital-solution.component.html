What you get

Foundation Twin provides a complete starter Digital Twin for terminals looking to begin digital monitoring. We develop a full 3D model of the terminal asset using AutoCAD or similar CAD tools and configure live dashboards based on the available site instrumentation and data points. This package gives your teams a clear terminal overview, performance KPIs, and loading area visibility for day to day operations.

Insight Twin is designed for terminals that require a more accurate and expandable Digital Twin. We develop an advanced 3D model using industrial platforms such as Hexagon or similar tools, with the option to include laser scanned inputs to align the model with as built site conditions. This package expands operational visibility for truck and marine operations and introduces structured preventive maintenance analytics through dashboards and analysis tables. KPI sets and reporting views can be tuned to match your operational priorities.

Immersive Twin delivers a premium Digital Twin experience with a VR ready model of the complete terminal asset. Along with live monitoring and advanced dashboards, this package enables immersive virtual walkthroughs for training and safer operational planning. It also adds process demonstration and simulation capability so teams can learn operating workflows and process sequences in a controlled environment. Equipment management is included, linking each equipment item to its location inside the model so users can quickly navigate to assets, view information, and support troubleshooting and inspections.
